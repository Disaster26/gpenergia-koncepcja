How it works

  1. Send part numbers or a BOM file (xlsx, csv, pdf) to info@gpenergia.com.pl or through the form.
  2. We check availability with distributors, manufacturers and independent suppliers across Europe.
  3. You get one quote: price, lead time, alternatives and delivery terms for every line.
  4. We consolidate everything at our warehouse and ship it as one delivery.

Manufacturer or distributor?

Buying direct from the manufacturer makes sense when a project needs the maker's full technical support, for example drive systems, SCADA or certified OEM applications. For prefabrication, switchboards, retrofits and export, a project distributor usually gives you more.

Frequently asked questions

What information do you need to quote my BOM?

For each line: manufacturer, full part number, short description and quantity. Add whether alternatives are allowed, any required certificates and the delivery address and date. An Excel or CSV file is easiest, but PDF or a scan works too.

Can you find parts that the manufacturer no longer makes?

Often, yes. We search remaining stock at distributors and independent suppliers in Europe. When the original is not available or not worth the risk, we propose a compatible alternative and send the documentation so your engineer can approve it.

Will you propose cheaper alternatives?

Only where it makes sense and only if you allow it. We mark every alternative clearly in the quote, next to the original part, so you decide line by line.

Which delivery terms do you offer?

EXW, FCA or DAP, from our warehouse in Długołęka near Wrocław. We ship by DHL, FedEx, UPS or pallet freight.
