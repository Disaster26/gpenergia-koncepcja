Project supply for panel builders, integrators and contractors

Switchboard prefabrication, plant retrofits and building system upgrades all depend on one thing: having every part on site when the schedule says so. We built switchboards and control cabinets ourselves for years, so we know which lines in a BOM usually cause delays.

What we take on

  • review of the project BOM and flagging of long-lead and discontinued items,
  • alternatives that keep the design compliant,
  • price optimisation across brands,
  • deliveries split into stages to match your schedule,

Who it is for

Switchboard prefabricators, automation integrators, OEMs, general contractors and plant owners, as well as distributors, resellers and procurement teams. Industries: industrial automation, switchboard prefabrication, machine building, food processing, marine, oil and gas, power generation and rail.
