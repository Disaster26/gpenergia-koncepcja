Have a BOM? We will quote all of it

A BOM (bill of materials) lists every component needed to build a control cabinet, a machine, a device or an installation. In switchboard prefabrication, retrofits and export projects, what matters is getting the whole list from one source, on time and at a sensible price.

Just send us the file. A well-prepared BOM includes:

  • the part number, e.g. 6ES7212-1BE40-0XB0,
  • a technical description and quantity,
  • the preferred brand, if one is required,
  • notes: whether alternatives are acceptable and which certificates you need.

We reply fast and often send a quote within 1-6 hours. We handle orders from EUR 100 up to BOMs worth several hundred thousand euros.

Manufacturer or distributor?

Buying direct from the manufacturer makes sense when a project needs the maker's full technical support, e.g. drive systems, SCADA or certified OEM applications. For prefabrication, switchboards, retrofits and export, a project distributor gives you more.
