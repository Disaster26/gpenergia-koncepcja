Send your BOM, get one quote for the whole list

A BOM (bill of materials) lists every component needed to build a control cabinet, a machine or an installation. In switchboard prefabrication, retrofits and export projects what matters is getting the whole list from one source, on time and at a sensible price.

A list we can quote quickly includes:

  • the part number, e.g. 6ES7212-1BE40-0XB0,
  • a short technical description and the quantity,
  • the preferred manufacturer, if one is required,
  • whether alternatives are acceptable and which certificates you need.

We handle orders from EUR 100 up to BOMs worth several hundred thousand euros.

Frequently asked questions

What does GP ENERGIA do?

GP ENERGIA sp. z o.o. is a Polish B2B supplier of industrial automation and electrical components. We source PLCs, HMIs, sensors, contactors, power supplies, cabinets and other parts to customer BOM lists and deliver them in Poland and abroad from our warehouse near Wrocław.

Can you quote a whole BOM with mixed brands?

Yes. Send the full list, even if it mixes Siemens, Schneider Electric, Phoenix Contact, Allen-Bradley or other brands. You get one quote with price, lead time and, where useful, a proposed alternative for every line.

How fast do you reply to an enquiry?

We often send a quote within 1-6 hours. Long or unusual lists can take longer, and we tell you when to expect the answer.

Is there a minimum order value?

We handle orders from about EUR 100 up to project BOMs worth several hundred thousand euros.

Do you ship outside the EU?

Yes. We ship across Europe and to the United States, Asia, the Middle East and Africa, by courier (DHL, FedEx, UPS) or pallet freight, under EXW, FCA or DAP.

Are the products genuine?

We supply original products from established manufacturers and tell you where each item comes from. If a part is no longer made, we say so and propose an alternative instead of offering doubtful stock.

Ask for price and availability

Send part numbers or attach your BOM file. We reply with prices, lead times and any alternatives, often within 1-6 hours.
